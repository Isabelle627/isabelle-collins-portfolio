AI-ASSISTED GRAPHIC DESIGN · 2026

Better Days Ahead.

An original album-cover concept about getting through difficult times and finding hope again.

THE IDEA

A moment of hope.

The fictional indie-pop/acoustic song “Better Days Ahead” is about resilience, patience, and realizing that life can improve. I used a person looking across a mountain valley at sunset to represent reflection and moving forward.

VISUAL DIRECTION

Warm, calm, reflective.

The palette combines deep blue, muted green, peach, golden orange, and soft cream. The open sky gives the title room to breathe, while the wildflowers and landscape add warmth and detail without overpowering the main image.

PROCESS & REFLECTION

Small changes matter.

The first direction felt too dark and the foreground lacked detail. I revised the prompt to brighten the sunset, add a lake and wildflowers, and simplify the typography. This helped the final design communicate a more welcoming and hopeful mood.

WHAT I LEARNED

Iterate with intention.

Specific instructions about lighting, foreground details, mood, and typography helped guide the AI toward the intended visual direction. Reviewing each version and revising it was more effective than accepting the first result.
